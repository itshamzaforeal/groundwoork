The approach

Insight is easy.
Follow-through is the work.

Most coaching ends at the whiteboard. Groundwork starts there.

You already know more about your situation than a 50-minute call can surface. What's usually missing isn't clarity — it's a plan sized to your actual week, and someone checking whether you kept it.

Sessions end with something written down: one commitment, specific enough to fail visibly. Between sessions, you send short field notes — two lines on what happened, not a journal. We use them to adjust the plan, not to make you feel behind.

Every six weeks we stop and recalibrate: what's working, what's quietly not, and whether the goal itself has changed. The map gets redrawn against the terrain, not the other way around.

How it works

A four-part arc, then a rhythm that's yours.

01

Discovery call

Thirty minutes, no pitch. We talk through where you're stuck and whether Groundwork is actually a fit — for both of us.

02

Mapping session

A 90-minute working session to turn a vague goal into a 90-day plan with real dates, obstacles named in advance, and a definition of done.

03

Weekly sessions

Fifty minutes, same time every week. We review what happened, solve what's actually in the way, and leave with one commitment.

04

Recalibration

Every six weeks, a longer session to check the plan against reality and adjust course before drift becomes a pattern.
